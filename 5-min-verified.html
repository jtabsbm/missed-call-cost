What this means

What the badge means: an independent 7-day audit of this business's callback log found that, during its posted business hours, the median missed call, web form, or message was answered by a human in under 5 minutes, 95% were answered in under 15 minutes, and not one went an hour without a response.

What it does not mean: it is not a guarantee, not a rank, not a review of the business's work — it certifies one measurable behavior over one week, and it is re-verified monthly and revocable. A business displaying this badge has a live directory entry proving its current status.
